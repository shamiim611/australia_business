AI adoption is
unevenly distributed.

About one in eight businesses reported AI use. Adoption differs sharply by business size and industry.

How was reported AI use distributed across Australian businesses in 2024–25? Published survey tables let us describe who reports using AI, while leaving the reasons for those differences open.

Reading these results

Each estimate links to its original workbook. Sheet and cell coordinates appear in the link tooltip and CSV exports. † marks caution, NP suppression, and !! an estimate too unreliable for charts or calculated comparisons. Missing values remain missing. Source-specific rounded zeros are identified.

All comparisons are descriptive. No regression, causal effect, confidence interval or significance claim is made. Exact uncertainty for the AI comparisons has not been established. Aggregate category percentages are not individual businesses, and separate industry and size margins cannot reconstruct joint cells.

The study does not estimate SME averages, adopter counts or weighted industry dispersion without compatible population weights. It does not infer AI-specific barriers from general ICT questions.
